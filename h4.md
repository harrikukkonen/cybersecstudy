## x) Why would you want to use a feed reader (and RSS/Atom)?
With feed reader and RSS/Atom feeds you can actually control what you read online. Instead of letting social media algorithms decide what shows up. You can get direct connection to the exact blogs, news sites, podcasts and forums that you actually are interested about and all in one place.

Source: https://simpsonian.ca/blog/whats-an-rss-reader-why-use-one/
## y) Summary Supreme court of Finland, case Osuuspankkikeskus port scan KKO:2003:36.

Case: 17 year old invidual used scanning software to perform port scan on the network of OPK to find open proxy servers. The scan did not breach the OPK firewalls. 

Ruling: The court ruled that the act met criteria for an attempted break-in. Systematically scanning for security vulnerabilities with software was understood as an attempt to obtain information how to bypass security measures. 

Damages: The 17 year old invidual was ordered to pay the investigation cost by the OPK and and its IT company(75 000 FIM) in full. 

Source: https://www.finlex.fi/fi/oikeuskaytanto/korkein-oikeus/ennakkopaatokset/2003/36
